Signal timing — the IC signal stack > a badge chase

For IC, your reliable signals are the artifact + the ML thesis (→ publication + letter) + open-source + a blog — more universal and more buildable-in-the-runway than a Kaggle badge you can't reach in time. Rank effort accordingly.
  • Codeforces — the one competitive lever. Individual; verify your account; aim OA-ready (Expert→CM as far as you get), peak summer 2028 before fall-2028 recruiting. It doubles as coding-to-par + the quant OA. Don't gate the plan on a specific number.
  • Kaggle — demoted to light. Master (1 gold + 2 silver) is the "gets-noticed" threshold but a multi-year stretch you likely won't hit in the runway — and it's your strength, not your gap. Do ONE comp for skill + a project writeup, not a badge grind.
  • Quant puzzles (Green Book / Zetamac) — defer to ~Spring 2028, only if quant goes live (your stats shortens the ramp). QuantFest (JS/Tower/HRT/IMC-sponsored CP+quant comp) = a targeted quant signal if you want one.
  • ICPC team medal — dropped (teammate-capped). Codeforces is the individual equivalent.
