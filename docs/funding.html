Funding — the money model

Verified 28 Aug 2026 (4-agent primary-source scan). Context: family support is ~€10,000 guaranteed, up to €30k possible but not assured, and the ability to produce a ~€20k lump sum at the start is the real doubt. Self-generated target: USD 10–15k by autumn 2028.

Headline: the lump-sum fear was oversized, and there are three separate ways to delete it. The German blocked account is €11,904 today (pegged to the BAföG rate; project €12.5–13.5k by 2028) — not €20k — and it is not a fee: it is your own year-one living money, released to you at €992/month. Your guaranteed €10k nearly covers it outright. And any of DAAD, Böll, EMJM or ESOP replaces the proof-of-funds requirement entirely.

Erasmus Mundus — the largest award on the board REACTIVATED

The August downgrade assumed a funded floor existed. It doesn't, so this comes back — and the numbers are bigger than anything else available.

  • €1,400/month × 24 = €33,600 cash + ~€18,000 participation costs waived + full insurance ≈ €51,600 of value. Consortia are forbidden from charging scholarship holders tuition or even an application fee. The award letter is the visa means-proof — and none of the host countries uses a German-style blocked account.
  • Three on-lane programmes exist right now: DEAI (Data Engineering & AI — ULB / UPC Barcelona / TU Wien / Lyon 1 / Padova) · InterMaths (L'Aquila / TUHH / TU Wien / UAB) · MATHS-DISC (mathematical modelling, simulation, data science). Application rounds run ~Nov–Feb; the 2028-entry rounds publish in late 2027.
  • Kazakhstan is on the EACEA NDICI "targeted regions" list (Central Asia) → access to an additional scholarship pool on top of the base allocation. And the 10%-per-nationality cap that throttles Indian and Nigerian applicants never binds for KZ.
  • Honest cost: forced annual mobility still fragments letter relationships and thesis continuity, and the partner universities are good-but-not-LMU-tier. It is a funding-first option, not a fit-first one — correctly ranked below LMU-with-DAAD, correctly ranked above self-funding anything.

Switzerland — survives only via ESOP

  • ETH ESOP: CHF 12,000/semester + full tuition waiver ≈ CHF 56,800. And the mechanism that matters: Canton Zürich accepts "a binding assurance of sufficient scholarships" in place of the CHF 21,000 cash proof. An ESOP letter closes the Swiss visa gate with zero family lump sum — so ETH, the "reach", is paradoxically the school where the money problem disappears completely rather than partially. ~60 awards across all of ETH; plan for a miss.
  • EPFL Master Excellence Fellowship: CHF 10,000/semester × 4 = CHF 40,000, applied for by ticking a box on the MSc application (15 Dec round).
  • EPFL's compulsory internship semester pays a cantonal-minimum CHF 2,000+/month — a real, structural offset the German programmes don't have.
  • ETH TA work: CHF 30.70/hour. ETH need-based scholarships: year 2 only, non-ETH-bachelor students must complete year 1 first.
