Summer 2027 — decision

Verdict: Tier 1 = Summer@EPFL + ETH SSRF (the ETH/EPFL touchpoint is the strategic prize); hold a remote-RA fallback; do NOT chase a quant-DESK internship this summer. amended 2026-08-26 — "no industry" was too broad: industrial research labs (Tier 3 below) do produce research letters and were never searched.

For a no-PhD candidate the research summer's payoff was never a PhD letter — it's the master-admission engine, and the Swiss master is the plan. It plays to your stats strength (more winnable now than an elite internship you can't yet clear on engineering), feeds ESOP mechanically (2 professor letters + a thesis pre-proposal), and hosts Artifact A3 + coding reps. The elite industry internship belongs to a later summer (during the master, ~Summer 2029), where it converts to a return offer.

The verified slate 3-agent primary-source scan, 26 Aug 2026

The headline is a calendar problem, not a shortage. Eleven programmes clear the filters — but four of the best land inside a six-week band (~1 Nov – mid-Dec 2026) that collides head-on with the artifact conversion + ReScience + ASA + CFE window. The escape hatch: a whole second window in Jan–Feb 2027 is collision-free, and the single best lane fit (KAUST) is rolling. Apply in waves, not all at once.
Application policy (revised 2026-08-27): the five filters (funded · English · undergrad-eligible · no nationality bar · timing) are feasibility gates. Field fit is NOT a gate — it is an odds weighting and a tiebreak among offers. Apply to everything that clears the gates, rank by odds × value, accept the best, decline the rest. Target ~8–10 applications (first costs ~6–10 h, each subsequent ~2–4 h of tailoring → ~35–40 h across Oct–Feb, inside the 6 h/wk outreach budget). The real cap is letters, not essays: one letter uploaded to N portals, so make ONE consolidated ask in October with the full programme list and deadlines — never a drip of requests. The exposure to manage is landing nothing, not landing something imperfect.
DURATION GATE — verified 27 Aug 2026 (2 agents). Available window = ~1 Jun → ~15 Sep 2027 (~15 wks): exams end mid-to-late May (⚠ exact date unknown until the UoL timetable publishes ~Feb–Mar 2027 — FIVE papers this session, so the LAST paper sets the start); overflow into the first two weeks of September is affordable, the third is not (the in-person document-ordering for the MSc applications starts ~15 Sep and has no backstop). Result: duration eliminates nothing — no programme has a minimum above the window. One casualty on other grounds:
  • CaCTüS — DEAD, remove from Tier 1. Fixed 90-day block 1 Jul–30 Sep with the closing symposium in late September; no version ends by 31 Aug. Separately it is an access/equity programme targeting students facing significant barriers to research — wrong fit twice over.
  • CS Max Planck — downgraded to TIGHT. Calendar works (12–14 wks, host-negotiated) but he would bid the shortest length in a pool where length is a selection advantage (European internship-semester students run 5–6 months), and March notification vs a June start makes the German visa tight.
  • ETH SSRF correction: CHF 4,000 is the total for two months, not monthly. Block is fixed 1 Jul–31 Aug — sits wholly inside the window with June as buffer.
  • KAUST is gated on two FIXABLE things, not dates: published minimum GPA 3.5/4.0 vs his 3.46, and a valid English test is mandatory while his IELTS is expired. KAUST wants the application ~mid-April 2027 (arrival ≥6 wks later), but the plan puts IELTS in June 2027 — a direct collision. To keep KAUST alive: lift the GPA this autumn AND move the IELTS to ~Feb–Mar 2027 (which lands on the exam ramp) or confirm an alternative medium-of-instruction proof.
  • Best calendar fit = Weizmann (fixed 8 wks, 17 Jun–13 Aug in 2026, deadline ~2 Feb) — slack at both ends absorbs an exam overrun. Best overall = Summer@EPFL (2–3 months negotiated with the lab, on-lane IC hosts, earliest decision of the slate → most visa-safe).

Verified dead ends — stop considering these

  • European industry research labs: the category is closed. 14 labs checked, exactly ONE survives — Google Student Researcher (EMEA), which does name Bachelor's and Statistics explicitly (window ~Oct–Nov 2026 to ~late Feb 2027; travel/relocation unclear). Everything else fails a hard gate: Naver Labs Europe (MSc/PhD + 5–6 months) · Bosch CAI (MSc/PhD plus a NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR-tier publication) · Amazon Tübingen (PhD/MSc) · IBM Zürich's funded scheme (Rensselaer/Notre Dame students only) · MSR Cambridge (UK university + work authorisation) · Toyota Motor Europe (EEA/Swiss citizens only) · JPMorgan MLCoE and Qualcomm (PhD) · Idiap (six-month minimum) · IDSIA (no programme exists).
  • A*STAR Singapore is gone. SIPGA — the only scheme that ever took overseas undergraduates — now 404s across every URL and is absent from both current index pages. ARIA is Singaporeans-only; CIARE is local-institution-only. Aggregator sites still advertising "SIPGA 2026" are stale copy.
  • Academia Sinica — dropped on funding (this corrects the 26-Aug entry). TIIP pays no accommodation and only half-capped travel in arrears; the ISS in-house internship is stipend-only with zero travel support and self-arranged dorms. ISS does have on-lane faculty, but none advertise covariance/shrinkage, and self-funding airfare + rent for a weak topical match fails the test.
  • OIST — declined on a duration collision (also corrects 26 Aug). Funding is genuinely best-in-class (housing free, round-trip airfare paid, deadline 15 Oct 2026) — but the minimum stay is 4 months, the Spring intake runs 1 Apr–30 Sep 2027, and May belongs to the exam session. The only legal shape is ~1 Jun–30 Sep 2027, which eats September 2027 — the month the application document pack starts. Also: its 15 Oct deadline is the MT1173 exam date, and the FAQ forbids contacting faculty before applying. Revisit only if the research summer is otherwise empty.
  • Also failed: Caltech SURF (visiting students are not funded — correct the Tier-1 list) · Mitacs Globalink (Kazakhstan is not on the eligible-country list) · Hong Kong programmes (fee-paying or unfunded) · Vector/Mila (no open funded undergrad programme) · POSTECH, IBS.
Fallback under everything: if no placement lands, a remote RA (a CH group or a KZ mentor) still builds the research record + letter, cheaper. The summer plan survives every branch.
⚠ Open requirement (logged 2026-08-26): KBTU requires a bachelor internship in summer 2027 — a graduation requirement the plan had not accounted for, landing in the zero-float letter window. A funded placement that also satisfies the requirement is the two-birds outcome and should drive the choice.
