Publish & Compete — the external-deadline lattice

Every venue, competition, and hard date — verified 2026-08-18 against primary sources. START ≤ date = when to begin, buffer included.

The four rules of this page.
  • One manuscript, many doors. Two documents exist: the reproduction write-up (Nov) and the 4–8-page extension paper (Dec–Jan). Every venue below is a routing of one of these two — never a third paper.
  • Crunch windows are sacred. Nothing on this page may touch Sep–mid-Oct 2026, Apr–May 2027, Sep–Dec 2027 (application season itself), or Apr–May 2028. Items landing near those windows carry a near-zero-work rule or get skipped.
  • Caps: puzzle/competition lane = 2 h/wk, always. Paper lane = 3–6 h/wk, Nov–Feb only, paid for by dropping Abbott. Exceeding a cap = the item gets cut, not the plan.
  • Binders: a deadline binds when a named person expects the thing — pre-announce every ship/submit date to someone specific.
